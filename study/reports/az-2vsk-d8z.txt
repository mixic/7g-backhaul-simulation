Traffic Manager – Intermittent Network Availability Issues - Mitigated

Tracking ID: 2VSK-D8Z

Summary of Impact: Between 09:00 UTC and 10:42 UTC on 10 November 2023, we identified that an issue affecting the Azure Traffic Manager (ATM) service manifested in intermittent availability issues which affected Azure and a set of Microsoft Services. Customers might have seen intermittent connectivity failures during this duration.
Preliminary Root Cause: We identified that a sudden spike in traffic resulted in the ATM service reaching transient operational thresholds. This manifested in the above-mentioned intermittent availability issues.
Mitigation: We changed the traffic management policy for the ATM service which successfully returned the services to a healthy state. Further monitoring of our telemetry confirms that the service is fully recovered, and this issue is mitigated.
Next Steps: We will continue to investigate to establish the full root cause and prevent future occurrences.
